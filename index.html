About

Most recently I was Engineering Lead in the Human Influence Team at the AI Security Institute, leading a team building research-automation tools to study the societal impacts of AI at scale. Before that, as a Research Engineer, I studied the effects of AI on people over long time horizons, for example in therapeutic or advice-giving settings (recent preprint summarising the group's interests).

Before AISI, I was Lead ML Scientist at the mental health startup Limbic; this Nature Medicine paper showcases some of my work.

Earlier, I was an academic in neuroscience and experimental psychology at Oxford, using deep reinforcement learning and neuroimaging to understand how people select and pursue conflicting goals. This paper best summarises my interests during that time.

In my spare time I enjoy writing fiction, and I am a writer-alumnus of the Faber Academy.
